BodyScope is now live on Google Play. It's a 3D human anatomy atlas for Android — 1,600+ anatomical structures across 11 body systems, rendered in real-time 3D, fully offline, no account, no ads, no subscription.

> **Download**: [Google Play — BodyScope](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.bodyscope.atlas)

![BodyScope — full body view](/images/bodyscope/01-full-body.png)

### Who It's For

Med students who need a fast reference between lectures. Nursing, PT, and chiropractic students learning the musculoskeletal system. Anatomy teachers who want a tool they can put on a phone or tablet without per-seat licensing. Artists studying figure drawing. Anyone who wants to poke around their own anatomy in 3D without paying $80/year for Complete Anatomy or signing up for an account that'll email them forever.

### What's Inside

- 1,600+ structures across 11 body systems — skeletal, muscular, cardiovascular, nervous, digestive, respiratory, urinary, reproductive, lymphatic, integumentary, endocrine
- Real-time 3D — pan, zoom, rotate, isolate any structure
- Search by name across every structure
- Quiz mode for spaced-repetition learners
- Fully offline once installed — no signup, no internet required, no ads

### Why I Built It

I'm a massage therapist by education. I missed having this tool when I was studying, so I built it for myself. Complete Anatomy is excellent but it's not free. The free alternatives I found were either web-only, ad-heavy, or didn't render in 3D.

It's free because I want it used, not monetized. If it saves you an hour of study time and you feel like throwing me a coffee — [the link is here](https://buymeacoffee.com/3qutj2ucoq). If you don't, that's fine too.

If you're a med, nursing, PT, art, or anatomy student — give it a try and let me know what's missing at andrii@shylenko.com.
