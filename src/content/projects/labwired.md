## What it is

Labwired is a public project area for tools and experiments around laboratory, embedded, and development workflows. It sits at the intersection of bench work, automation, and the repeatable developer workflows needed to keep engineering tasks maintainable.

## Why it matters

Embedded and laboratory work often depends on repeated setup, measurement, and documentation steps. Labwired frames those repeated tasks as tooling problems, so the workflow can become easier to run, review, and improve over time.

## Technical judgment

The project demonstrates a bias toward turning ad hoc engineering routines into maintainable software. It also shows product judgment around developer experience: useful tooling has to fit the way engineers already work, not only expose the underlying technical capability.
