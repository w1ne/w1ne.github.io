An Android app for recording video with two DJI Mic Mini mics. Each mic gets its own audio channel, so you can adjust the voices separately when editing. Works offline, without a receiver.

Pair the mics, open the app and tap Record. Stop saves the video.

- [GitHub](https://github.com/w1ne/stereo-bridge)

- [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.stereobridge)
