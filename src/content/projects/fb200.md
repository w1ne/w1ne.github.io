The FLAMMA FB200 is a small bass multi-effects pedal with a 600 MHz Cortex-M7 inside. Its firmware is closed. I wrote an open replacement that sounds the same as the stock one and adds what I missed: updates over USB with no button combo, a debug console, crash reports, clearer display messages, drums in the USB recording, and live drum control.

You flash it from the browser. Nothing to install.

- [Flash it in the browser](https://shylenko.com/fb200-tools/flash.html)
