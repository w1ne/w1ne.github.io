## What it is

BodyScope is a 3D human anatomy atlas for Android. It renders 1,600+ structures across 11 body systems in real-time 3D — pan, zoom, isolate, search by name — fully offline after install. No ads, no subscription, no account.

## Why it matters

I'm a massage therapist by education. I missed having a tool like this when I was studying, so I built it for myself. Complete Anatomy is good but it's not free, and the free alternatives I found were either web-only, ad-heavy, or didn't render in 3D.
